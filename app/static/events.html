Monitoring policy

Acceleration follows a reviewed impact or wording-discrepancy notice that you explicitly mark for faster monitoring. Quieting follows the time since the last escalation. The event worker allows twelve started runs across all dossiers per US Central calendar day. Paused services and queued operator questions can delay research. Running work is allowed to finish.

Link an existing case or board

Links are checked against existing records. Event monitoring uses its own research requisition and does not automatically bind that requisition to the linked case or board.

Event timeline

Only reviewed groupings appear here. Dates are copied from the saved When field. A complete ISO date is sortable only if that exact date also occurs in the saved source narrative. Missing years are never inferred. “Added” is a dossier intake time, not an incident date or original collection time.

Reported changes & review cues

Numerical differences can reflect page edits or extraction differences. A cue does not verify a contradiction, an impact, or a change in risk level. Reviewed notices can appear in notes-enabled PDF briefs.

Monitoring runs

Latest thirty jobs. Completed research may yield no confirmed saved notes. Imported notes remain candidates until grouping review. Interrupted external-write runs are held for review rather than replayed.
