Research requisitions to watch

Each selected topic starts from its latest completed regular run as a baseline. Future completed runs are compared with that baseline; enabling does not replay historical alerts or start initial research. Existing collection schedules must be enabled separately. Own follow-up runs are excluded from detection. If several runs finish between checks, the newest is compared with the last baseline.

Automatic follow-ups are limited to reported evidence gaps and changes to saved source wording. Additional URLs and saved notes are informational only. Priority is a review category, not a threat rating. Numerical cues are not verified contradictions. No semantic contradiction analysis or source-independence verification is performed.

Follow-up queue

At most 20 jobs may be waiting or active. Automatic waiting jobs expire after seven days. One workflow per notice; unchanged gaps do not create repeated jobs. Follow-ups use the original topic and its existing board/case assignments. Confirmed saved notes flow through the existing delivery pipeline. A completed run may still have no usable sources or saved notes. Failed or interrupted external-write workflows are held for review rather than blindly rerun. Pause cancels waiting automatic work; active runs may finish.
