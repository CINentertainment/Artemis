Recovery controls

Service failures are checked again after increasing delays, up to ten minutes. Worker recovery starts after a ninety-second startup grace and allows three restart attempts per worker per hour. Running work is never interrupted to answer a new question. Queued voice questions take priority at the next available research slot; market work yields between symbols.

This monitor does not start external service processes or replay uncertain research writes. Use your normal startup tools if Ollama, SearXNG or another application has stopped. Pausing this monitor restores the existing workers’ normal behavior; it does not pause their schedules.
