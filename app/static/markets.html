Autonomous market research

US stocks, ETFs and selected major USD cryptocurrency pairs.

Discovery & monitoring

Enable once to discover provider-confirmed symbols, add them to Market Lens, refresh completed daily prices, collect issuer-linked reporting and retain dated directional outlooks. Discovery combines rotating sector searches with provider lookup. It is a bounded watchlist, not a scan of every listing. Starter symbols SPY, QQQ, BTC-USD and ETH-USD are benchmarks/coverage seeds, not recommendations.

Up to 50 symbols are tracked. Each cycle researches a rotating batch; prices refresh when that asset is processed. Artemis, Market Lens, Ollama and SearXNG must remain running. Research shares the existing research lock with World Watch and direct reports. Pausing prevents the next asset; an active request may finish.

Research notes & directional outlooks
Potential rise = bullish; potential fall = bearish. Neutral has no clear directional bias. Insufficient evidence is a valid result. All directional assessments are low-confidence, uncalibrated hypotheses; copied source evidence is separate from model reasoning. No trades, paper positions or price targets are created.
After the calendar horizon, observed direction uses a ±1% neutral band and the first stored session within seven days of the horizon. Comparison uses the latest adjusted-close series when available; revised history may change it. Missing prices remain unresolved. These selected outlooks are not a backtest of the whole market, and their match counts are not calibrated probabilities.
