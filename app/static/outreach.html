1 · Connect the business mailbox once

Uses business@cinentertainment.net and its verified send-as alias artemis@cinentertainment.net. Gmail access is separate from Gumroad and LinkedIn.

Google Workspace setup
  1. In your own Google Cloud project, enable Gmail API and configure an Internal OAuth audience for your Workspace organization where available.
  2. Create a Desktop app OAuth client and download its JSON. Keep the file local and out of Git.
  3. Run scripts\Connect-Artemis-Mailbox.ps1 from PowerShell. Supply that JSON path when prompted.
  4. In Google's authorization window, sign in as the business mailbox owner and grant the displayed sending, mailbox-read and alias-verification scopes.
  5. Return here and click Connect saved mailbox. No mail is sent by connection.

The mailbox-read permission covers the mailbox, not only the alias. This app retains matching outreach conversations; tokens are protected using Windows user encryption. Your Workspace administrator may need to approve access.

2 · Standing outreach policy

Introductions run Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM America/Chicago. One follow-up is eligible after five business days, only with no observed response. One prospect per business domain; no guessed emails or purchased lists. Toolkit and custom public-source research introductions are distributed across eligible prospects, rather than pitching each person twice.

Research services cover public-source business questions, industry developments and emerging risks. Initial mail invites a scoping conversation; it does not agree to fees, deadlines or deliverables. Any observed reply—including an opt-out, bounce or out-of-office message—stops the sequence. A manual send from your mailbox stops follow-ups too.

Artemis checks published same-domain business role addresses, relevant activity wording and an explicit US location. This is a heuristic prospecting rule, not proof of buying intent or a guarantee that a business welcomes promotion. Each introduction identifies CIN Studios and its commercial purpose and provides reply-to opt-out instructions.

Replies & handoffs

Review received content as external correspondence. Drafts are suggestions. The send button approves exactly the displayed text for the latest received message; a newer message invalidates that approval. You can also reply directly in Gmail and mark the conversation handled.
