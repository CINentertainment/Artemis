1 · Connect your publishing accounts

Use an approved LinkedIn developer application and a member access token. These are separate from your Gumroad credentials. Store secrets only here, locally.

Connection requirements and one-time setup
  1. Create a LinkedIn developer application associated with a Page you administer.
  2. For your profile, request Share on LinkedIn and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect. Generate a member token with openid profile w_member_social.
  3. For a company Page, LinkedIn must grant Community Management access. The token also needs w_organization_social and organization read/admin access for role and identity checks. A company Page must exist, and you need an approved administrator or content administrator role.
  4. Use the application's Auth tab/token tools to obtain an access token, Client ID and Client Secret. Enter these below. Verify each channel separately; no post is sent during verification.
  5. Confirm the returned name and identity, then select a standing policy. Tokens expire: reconnection may occasionally require you. This package does not operate your LinkedIn password or automate browser login.

Missing Page API approval cannot be solved by installation. The profile channel can operate independently when its permissions are available. Only official APIs are used.

Credentials are encrypted with the existing Windows user protection. Verification confirms identity, expiry and relevant scopes. A successful first publish is still the end-to-end API test. Disconnecting removes local credentials; revoke grants in LinkedIn to invalidate them there.

2 · Set the standing campaign policy

Approve the campaign scope once. Automatic mode publishes its exact factual copy without a separate review for each post. Draft mode retains a per-post approval step.

16:00 UTC is 10:00 AM US Central during standard time and 11:00 AM during daylight time. Three posts per week per enabled channel; the Page is staggered by 30 minutes. Missed windows older than one hour are skipped, not backfilled.

Review the complete 48-topic campaign for both channels

Permitted content: the displayed workflow tips, toolkit features, and the toolkit link. No saved intelligence, private notes, market predictions, testimonials, customer data, direct messages, replies, ads, refunds, or price changes. Search snippets never enter post copy. The campaign stops after 48 distinct posts per channel instead of repeating them forever.

Commerce must have a recent completed sync confirming the toolkit is published. Download delivery remains unverified from the skipped checkout test; enabling this policy does not mark it verified.

Publishing queue & results

Successful provider acknowledgements have a post link. Interrupted or ambiguous sends pause the channel. Check the feed and resolve the saved item before re-enabling it; Artemis never blindly retries an uncertain send.

Audience & resource research

Daily search results are triaged by simple, visible snippet rules. “Possible discussion venue” means only that its snippet mentions a forum, community or discussion. Contacts, buying intent and permission to promote remain unknown. No outreach is sent.
